Mateja Kvaternik Zupan

Architect | Photographer | Poet | Traveler

About

Mateja Kvaternik Zupan is a Slovenian architect, photographer, and poet. Born on January 1, 1965, in Ljubljana, she has dedicated her career to architectural heritage while expressing her creativity through photography and poetry.

She was born into a family of three children to France Kvaternik, a renowned architect of sacral architecture who significantly influenced her path. After graduating from the Faculty of Architecture in 1991 with a thesis on the renovation of the old Kaštel and revitalization of Grožnjan, she worked as an independent architect. During this time, she collaborated with her father on numerous heritage renovation projects (Grahovo, Žužemberk, Liboje) and created stained glass artworks.

Her career led her to the Ministry of the Environment and Spatial Planning, and later to the Government Office for Development and European Cohesion Policy. Since 2007, she has served at the Ministry of Culture as an Inspector – Senior Councillor for Cultural Heritage.

Mateja Kvaternik Zupan je slovenska arhitektka, fotografinja in pesnica. Rojena 1. 1. 1965 v Ljubljani, je svojo kariero posvetila kulturni dediščini, svojo ustvarjalnost pa izraža skozi fotografijo in poezijo.

Rojena je bila v družini s tremi otroki očetu Francetu Kvaterniku, priznanemu arhitektu sakralne arhitekture, ki je pomembno vplival na njeno pot. Po diplomi na Fakulteti za arhitekturo leta 1991 z nalogo o prenovi starega Kaštela in revitalizaciji Grožnjana, je delovala kot samostojna arhitektka. V tem času je z očetom sodelovala pri številnih prenovah kulturne dediščine (Grahovo, Žužemberk, Liboje) in se ukvarjala z vitraji.

Kasneje se je zaposlila na Ministrstvu za okolje in prostor, nato v Službi Vlade RS za evropska sredstva. Od leta 2007 je zaposlena na Ministrstvu za kulturo kot inšpektorica – višja svetnica za kulturno dediščino.

Poetry & Photography

She began writing poetry in her youth and developed a passion for photography during her travels. Her works combine her poetic voice with her authorized photographs.
